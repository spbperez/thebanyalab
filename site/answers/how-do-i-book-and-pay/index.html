How do I book, and how do I pay?

Send an application through this site. A master replies, confirms a date with you, and sends a payment link from the business — card, receipt, no cash in an envelope.

There is no open calendar on this site, on purpose. Sessions are small, the masters are few, and the ocean has an opinion about the schedule. So you send an application with roughly when suits you and how many of you there are, and a master comes back to you with a time.

Once the time is agreed you get a payment link. It is a card payment to the business, it produces a real receipt, and you can pay for yourself and for the people you are bringing in one go. Nothing is charged before a human has confirmed your date.

If you want to give a session to someone else, say so in the application and we will send the same link with a note instead of a date.
