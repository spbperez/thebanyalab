The Lab

Most banyas abroad replicate a fixed template — the same parilka, the same temperature, the same protocol, every time. We respect the tradition, and we do not stop there.

In a country the size of Russia the ritual split into hundreds of local variations — Siberian, Altai, Karelian, Ural, village traditions that never reached a textbook. Our masters come from that living tradition and keep working inside it. We read old practitioner notes. We travel back to masters who carry local knowledge. We test, we keep what works, we discard what does not.

That is what Lab means. Same ancient foundation. Active experimentation on top of it.
