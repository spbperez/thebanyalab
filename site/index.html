01 — Heat

The stone remembers

Steam here is not made by a machine. It is a ladle of water thrown onto stones heated for hours — heavy enough to flash the water into a fine steam, and to do it again a minute later without going cold. The heaters in a gym hold a fraction of that stone, and most of them forbid water on it altogether.

02 — Par

The venik is a tool

Air against your skin sits still and insulates you — that is why a body survives 200°F at all. Every sweep of oak breaks that layer and drives the heat in. Held high it takes the hottest air from the ceiling. Pressed flat it holds heat against the back. Lifted and fanned it cools. The master spends the round deciding where the heat goes.
